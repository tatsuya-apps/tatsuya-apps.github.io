Frequently asked questions

Mitayo — a tracker for the movies, dramas and anime you watch. Updated November 28, 2026

Getting started

Does it cost anything?

No. Mitayo is completely free, with no ads and no paid features.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up, email or password. Just open the app and start.

Is it available on Android or a computer?

Mitayo is for iPhone and iPad only.

Your records and backups

Where are my records stored?

On your iPhone and in your own iCloud. There is no Mitayo server, and the developer can’t see your records.

Will I lose my records when I get a new iPhone?

No. If you sign in with the same Apple ID, they come back from iCloud automatically. Check that Mitayo is turned on in Settings → your name → iCloud.

How do I make a backup?

Use “Export records (CSV)” in Mitayo’s settings to save a file. You can import it back into Mitayo later.

Can I use the same records on my iPad?

Yes. With the same Apple ID, iCloud keeps them in sync.

Netflix viewing history

How do I import what I’ve watched on Netflix?
  1. In Mitayo’s settings, tap “Import from Netflix” → “Open Netflix viewing activity”
  2. Tap “Download all” at the very bottom of the Netflix page
  3. Go back to Mitayo, tap “Choose the downloaded file” and pick “NetflixViewingHistory.csv”
  4. Tap “Match titles” → “Import automatically”

From the second time on, only newly watched episodes are added. Importing about once a month keeps your history up to date.

Titles from someone else’s profile were imported

Netflix keeps viewing history per profile. Switch to your own profile before downloading. You can remove an import all at once with “Undo import” in settings.

Title information

A title, overview or streaming service is wrong

Title data comes from TMDB (themoviedb.org) and streaming data from JustWatch. Anyone can edit TMDB, so fixing it there will show up in Mitayo after a while.

I can’t find a title

You can search by part of the title, cast and crew, character names or keywords. If it still doesn’t show up, use “Enter the title manually”.

The seasons or episode numbers look wrong

On the title page, tap “Change season grouping” to choose TMDB seasons, another grouping, absolute numbering, or your own. Your progress stays the same when you change it.

Everyone’s ranking

What is sent?

Only title IDs, your status (watched, want, etc.), stars, the week you watched, the subscription you used and your country. Your name, notes and Apple ID are never sent, and no one can tell whose records they are.

How do I stop taking part?

Turn it off anytime in settings. Turning it off also deletes what you’ve sent (it’s removed from the ranking counts within a week).

Notifications

I’m not getting notifications

Allow notifications in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Mitayo. You can also turn each type on or off under “Notifications” in Mitayo’s settings.

Deleting your records

How do I delete everything?

To delete one title, use “Delete this title” at the bottom of its page. To delete everything, delete Mitayo from your iPhone, then go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Mitayo and delete its data.
